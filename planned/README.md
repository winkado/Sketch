## Indeedee-F — redirect for the fast mode, terrain for Alakazam's Expanding Force
```
Indeedee-F @ Psychic Seed
Ability: Psychic Surge
Level: 50
EVs: 32 HP / 32 Def / 2 SpD
Bold Nature
- Heal Pulse
- Fake Out
- Follow Me
- Protect
```
Role: replaces Sinistcha in the fast bring beside Alakazam-Mega. Psychic Surge on entry blocks priority into grounded allies
(Sucker Punch, Aqua Jet, Extreme Speed, Fake Out — ability-independent, so Mold Breaker can't bypass it) and turns Expanding
Force into a 1.3x spread. Follow Me is not a powder move, so it redirects Grass-type attackers Rage Powder can't.
Checks when M-C lands: Indeedee-F in pokemon.csv; Psychic Surge retained; Follow Me legal on the F forme; Psychic Seed in items.csv
(absent from M-B — fall back to Sitrus Berry / Focus Sash if still absent); Heal Pulse legal.
Known holes it does not fix: spread damage into the setter (nothing heals faster than turn-1 Heat Wave), Kowtow Cleave into it (2x).
